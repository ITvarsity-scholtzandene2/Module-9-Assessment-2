Forno Vecchio Naples, Italy

Rating: ★★★★★

The restaurant Forno Vecchio is loud, joyful and full of life, exactly what a Naples pizzeria should be. The family who run it treat every guest like a cousin, and the service was quick and friendly even though every table was full. The atmosphere is wonderfully chaotic, with the glow of the wood-fired oven at the centre of it all. Prices are very affordable, which makes it outstanding value for some of the best food in the city.

Starter: Bruschetta with tomato, garlic and fresh basil

Simple but perfect. The bread was toasted until crunchy and rubbed with fresh garlic, then topped with sweet, juicy tomatoes and fragrant basil. A drizzle of good olive oil brought it all together.

Main: Wood-fired Margherita pizza with buffalo mozzarella, paired with limonata

This is the pizza I’ll be dreaming about for years. The crust was light and airy, with beautiful charred spots from the blazing oven. The tomato sauce was sweet and bright, and the creamy buffalo mozzarella melted into soft, rich pools. The sparkling limonata was the ideal partner, with its crisp, lemony fizz refreshing my palate between each delicious slice.

Dessert: Classic tiramisu

Light, creamy and perfectly balanced, this tiramisu was everything a classic should be. The coffee-soaked sponge was soft but not soggy, and the mascarpone cream was smooth and delicate. A dusting of cocoa finished it beautifully.
